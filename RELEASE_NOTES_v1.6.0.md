## 🎯 Features

### Boone & Crockett Scoring System
The biggest addition to Iron Stag yet! Now get **unofficial Boone & Crockett scores** for your harvested bucks:

- **Comprehensive scoring** – Support for Typical and Non-Typical Whitetail and Mule Deer
- **Detailed measurements** – Track main beams, G-points (G1-G7), H-points, and spread credits
- **Smart calculations** – Automatic gross, net, and final score computation with symmetry analysis
- **Accuracy warnings** – Get alerts for asymmetry and scoring considerations
- **Permanent records** – All your B&C scores are saved and accessible anytime

*Note: These are unofficial scores for personal record-keeping and should not be used for official B&C entry*

### Learn Section - Hunting Education Hub
Master the art of deer hunting with our new comprehensive education center:

- **Body condition analysis** – Learn to assess deer health and vitality
- **Antler development guide** – Understand growth patterns, mass, and symmetry
- **Behavioral indicators** – Recognize signs of deer behavior and movement patterns  
- **Ethical hunting principles** – Fair chase guidelines and responsible hunting practices
- **Land stewardship** – Conservation and habitat management insights

### Enhanced Scan Results Experience
We've completely redesigned how you view your scan results:

- **Bold recommendation display** – HARVEST or PASS shown prominently at the top
- **Clear confidence indicators** – High, Moderate, or Low confidence in easy-to-understand language
- **"Why We Think This" section** – Detailed breakdown of the AI's analysis reasoning
- **Scannable insights** – Key bullet points extracted for quick reference
- **Improved messaging** – More supportive and educational tone throughout

### Share Your Success
Show off your hunting success with our new sharing feature:

- **Professional scan cards** – Beautiful, branded share images with your scan results
- **Native sharing** – Use your phone's built-in share options
- **Multiple platforms** – Share via Messages, Email, Social Media, and more
- **Instant generation** – Create and share in seconds

### Advanced Offline Capabilities
Even better offline scanning when you're deep in the field:

- **Improved queue management** – Better visibility into pending scans
- **Smarter auto-sync** – Automatic upload when you return to signal
- **Robust error handling** – Better recovery from network issues
- **Persistent storage** – Your queued scans are safe even if the app closes

### Scan Organization & Tagging
Keep your hunting memories organized:

- **Custom tags** – Create and apply your own tags to categorize scans
- **Favorite scans** – Mark your best deer for easy access
- **Enhanced filtering** – Find specific scans quickly with improved search
- **Better history** – Improved scan history management and navigation
