## ✨ New Features

### Multi-Photo Scans
- **Up to 3 photos per scan** – Add several photos of the same deer to one scan for a better estimate
- **One scan credit** – A multi-photo scan uses only one credit, no matter how many photos you add
- **Crop before you scan** – A new crop tool lets you frame the deer before analysis
- **Photo tray** – Review, reorder, or remove photos before you submit
- **Combined results** – Iron Stag checks every photo and merges them into one result, and shows which photo gave which evidence

### Honest Age Ranges
- **Ranges when the evidence calls for it** – When the photos can't separate two age classes, you now see a range (for example, *3.5–4.5*) instead of a forced single number
- **Open-ended estimates** – Mature deer can show as *5.5+* when the exact upper age can't be told apart
- **Clear reasoning** – Each range explains why it was given
- **Ranges everywhere** – Scan results, history, and summary cards all show the range

### Useful "Can't Tell" Results
- **Stops on poor photos** – When a photo is too blurry, too dark, too far, or the deer is blocked, Iron Stag says it can't estimate the age instead of guessing
- **Tips to fix it** – You see the reason and tips for a better photo, so your next scan works

---

## 🎯 Accuracy & Transparency

### Clearer Confidence
- **Two confidence scores** – Confidence in the exact age is now separate from confidence in the age range, so you know how much to trust each
- **No more fixed deductions** – Confidence no longer drops by set amounts. We built a new system that will base scores on real results from deer of known age
- **Safe rollout** – New confidence settings are tested quietly in the background against current scores before they go live. Your scores stay the same in this release

### Accuracy Testing
- **Known-age test set** – Every change to the AI is now checked against deer of known age before it reaches you
- **Exact-age and range tracking** – We measure both how often the exact age is right and how often the true age falls inside the range

## 🔧 Improvements

- Clearer layout for uncertain or mixed results on the scan result screen
- History cards now show age ranges and uncertainty warnings
- Better consistency when several photos of the same deer disagree
