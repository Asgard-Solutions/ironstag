# Iron Stag - Billing & Subscription FAQ

## Overview
Answers to common billing and subscription questions for Iron Stag, including upgrades, cancellations, and receipts.

## Key Information
- Subscriptions are purchased through the App Store (iOS) or Play Store (Android); web/Expo Go uses Stripe checkout.
- Cancel anytime; access continues until the end of the billing period.
- Refunds are handled by Apple/Google according to their policies.
- Free scans do not refresh; upgrade is required after 3 lifetime scans.

## Step-by-Step
1. Open Profile and tap the subscription card.
2. Choose Monthly or Annual.
3. Complete purchase in the App Store or Play Store (or Stripe checkout on web/Expo Go).
4. Manage or cancel your subscription:
   - iOS: Settings → Your Name → Subscriptions → Iron Stag
   - Android: Play Store → Menu → Subscriptions → Iron Stag
   - Web/Stripe: Use Profile → Manage Subscription

## Common Questions
**Q: How do I upgrade?**
A: Go to Profile, tap the subscription card, and complete the purchase through the App Store/Play Store or Stripe checkout (web/Expo Go).

**Q: How do I restore purchases?**
A: On iOS/Android, tap **Restore Purchases** in Profile.

**Q: How do I cancel?**
A: Cancel in your device’s subscription settings (iOS Settings → Subscriptions or Google Play → Subscriptions).

**Q: I subscribed on web. How do I manage billing?**
A: Use Profile → Manage Subscription to open the Stripe portal.

**Q: Can I get a refund?**
A: Refunds are handled by Apple/Google according to their policies.

**Q: What happens if I cancel?**
A: You keep access until the end of your billing period, then revert to the Tracker tier.
