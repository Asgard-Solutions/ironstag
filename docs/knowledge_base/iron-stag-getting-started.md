# Iron Stag - Getting Started

## Overview
A first-time setup guide for installing Iron Stag, creating an account, and preparing your app for accurate analysis.

## Key Information
- Iron Stag requires an internet connection for AI analysis (offline scans are queued).
- You must accept the legal disclaimer on first launch.
- Setting your Home State improves regional calibration.

## Step-by-Step
1. Install Iron Stag from the App Store (iOS) or Google Play Store (Android).
2. Open the app and review the welcome screen.
3. Create an account or sign in.
   - iOS users can also use Sign in with Apple
4. Accept the legal disclaimer.
5. Grant camera and photo library access when prompted.
6. Set your Home State for regional calibration.
7. Start your first scan.

## Common Questions
**Q: Do I need an account to scan?**
A: Yes. You must create an account or sign in before scanning.

**Q: Why should I set my Home State?**
A: It enables regional calibration to make confidence scores more accurate for deer in your area.
