# Iron Stag - Password Reset & Account Recovery

## Overview
Reset your Iron Stag password using the email-based reset code sent to your registered email address.

## Key Information
- Reset codes are delivered by email.
- Reset codes expire after 15 minutes.

## Step-by-Step
1. Tap "Forgot Password?" on the login screen.
2. Enter your registered email address.
3. Tap "Send Reset Code."
4. Check your email for the 6-digit code.
5. Enter the code and your new password.
6. Tap "Reset Password."

## Common Questions
**Q: How long is the reset code valid?**
A: Reset codes expire after 15 minutes.

**Q: I didn't receive a code. What should I do?**
A: Double-check that you entered the correct email address, then try again.
