# Iron Stag - Pricing & Plans

## Overview
A quick summary of Iron Stag's free and paid subscription tiers, pricing, and what each plan includes.

## Key Information
- **Tracker (Free):** 3 lifetime scans, full AI analysis with regional calibration, scan history, local image storage.
- **Master Stag (Premium):** $9.99/month or $89.99/year.
- **Master Stag features:** unlimited scans, regional calibration, scan history with search/filter, edit and re-analyze scans, favorites/tags, optional feedback labels, priority support, all future features included.
- Free scans do not refresh; once used, you must upgrade to continue scanning.

## Step-by-Step
1. Use all 3 free scans, or open Profile and tap the subscription card.
2. Select Monthly ($9.99/mo) or Annual ($89.99/yr).
3. Complete purchase through the App Store or Play Store (or Stripe checkout on web/Expo Go).
4. Subscription activates immediately.

## Common Questions
**Q: Do free scans refresh?**
A: No. Free users get 3 lifetime scans. Once used, upgrade is required.

**Q: What's the difference between monthly and annual?**
A: Annual ($89.99/yr) saves $30 compared to monthly ($9.99/mo × 12 = $119.88/yr).

**Q: What happens if I cancel?**
A: You keep access until the end of your billing period, then revert to the Tracker tier.
