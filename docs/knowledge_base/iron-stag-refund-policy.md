# Iron Stag - Refund Policy

## Overview
Refunds for Iron Stag subscriptions are handled by Apple or Google based on their store policies. Web purchases via Stripe follow Stripe’s refund process.

## Key Information
- Refunds are processed by the App Store or Play Store according to their policies.
- Stripe refunds are handled via the Stripe customer portal or support.
- You can cancel anytime; access continues until the end of your billing period.
- No refunds for partial billing periods are noted in current documentation.

## Step-by-Step
1. For refund requests, follow the App Store or Play Store refund process.
2. For cancellations, manage your subscription in device settings:
   - iOS: Settings → Your Name → Subscriptions → Iron Stag
   - Android: Play Store → Menu → Subscriptions → Iron Stag
3. For Stripe purchases, use Profile → Manage Subscription to open the Stripe portal.

## Common Questions
**Q: Can Iron Stag issue a refund directly?**
A: App Store and Play Store refunds are handled by Apple/Google. Stripe refunds are handled via Stripe or support.

**Q: What happens if I cancel?**
A: You keep access until the end of your billing period.
