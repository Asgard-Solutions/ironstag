# Iron Stag - System Requirements

## Overview
Minimum device and OS requirements for running Iron Stag on iOS and Android.

## Key Information
- **iOS:** iOS 15.1 or later; iPhone 8 or newer recommended.
- **Android:** Android 7.0 (API 24) or later; 4GB RAM recommended.
- Camera access is required; internet is required for analysis (offline scans are queued).

## Step-by-Step
1. Verify your device meets the OS requirements above.
2. Ensure camera access is enabled.
3. Confirm you have a stable internet connection for analysis.

## Common Questions
**Q: Does Iron Stag work offline?**
A: AI analysis requires internet, but scans can be queued offline and uploaded automatically when you’re back online.

**Q: Is there a mobile app?**
A: Yes. Iron Stag is available on iOS and Android.
